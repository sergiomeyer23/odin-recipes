Brigadeiro

Brigadeiro is the sweet you will find at every birthday party in Brazil. It is a chocolate fudge ball rolled in sprinkles, and it only needs three ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 3 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter, plus a little extra for your hands
  • Chocolate sprinkles for rolling

Steps

  1. Put the condensed milk, cocoa powder and butter in a pan over low heat.
  2. Stir all the time with a spoon. After about 10 minutes the mixture gets thick and you can see the bottom of the pan when you drag the spoon.
  3. Pour it onto a buttered plate and let it cool completely.
  4. Butter your hands and roll small balls of the mixture.
  5. Roll each ball in the sprinkles and put it in a paper cup.

Makes about 25 brigadeiros. Keep them in the fridge for up to 3 days.
